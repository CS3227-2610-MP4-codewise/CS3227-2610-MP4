## Findings returned to owner

1. **F1 — UI-07, narrow conflict page overflow (medium).** At 320 px, both light/dark conflict views produce `documentElement.scrollWidth = 360`; normal/resolved editor returns to 320. Reproduce with `review.html?width=320&theme=light&state=conflict-fallback` or the dark conflict scenario. The diff is a labeled local scroll region, but additional document overflow violates the contract. [Dark screenshot](ui-review/screenshots/320-dark-conflict.png), [light measurement](ui-review/metrics-320-light-conflict-fallback-motionfalse-__Unsaved_copy_found__Choose_a_version_.json). Fix the escaping content and recheck the diff plus both confirmation/copy branches; merely hiding document overflow would not establish reachability.
2. **F2 — UI-07, undersized targets (medium).** Measured CSS boxes: Back to the assignment **190.8 × 18 px**; session Sign in **76.8 × 37.5 px**; Skip to content **137.8 × 42.5 px**. Main actions, question/rubric disclosures, role label controls and answer tabs generally meet 44 px; narrow tabs are 144 × 52 px, navigation/confirmation buttons 288 × 44 px. Correct target padding/minimum size while preserving narrow layout. [Session measurements](ui-review/metrics-1440-light-session-__Not_saved__Your_session_has_ended_.json), [narrow controls](ui-review/metrics-320-light-conflict-fallback-motionfalse-__Unsaved_copy_found__Choose_a_version_.json).
3. **F3 — UI-07/UI-08, focus lost during conflict replacement (medium).** Activating Keep my version or Use saved version removes the focused trigger and leaves focus on the document rather than a logical control in Confirm choice. Confirming either choice similarly removes the focused confirmation without focusing the editor. Observed in native AX focus output at 320/1024 px, and consistent with `ConflictWindow` conditionally replacing controls without a focus effect. Next-question navigation correctly focuses its heading, so that behavior should be preserved. Focus the first appropriate confirmation action/heading, then restore focus to the editor after resolution; recheck Back and clipboard fallback.
